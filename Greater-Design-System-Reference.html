The Laws

Ink-forward (1.6). Primary actions & active navigation are ink (near-black; inverts to white in dark). Blue — --p-primary — is reserved for links, focus, and selection/state inside content. In Black Ops, "blue" is the teal #86EAE8; the law is unchanged.
Humans read Inter; the machine speaks mono (1.12, litmus 1.12.2). A destination or descriptor a human reads → Inter. A control, datum, or the machine addressing the operator → mono. Mono sizes land on an integer scale (10/11/12/13/14). 1.13 narrows it: page H1s are Inter via --font-h1 (Black Ops re-monos everything except table cells — IBM Plex via --font-cell).
The Badge Vocabulary Law (1.13). Stroke-outline styling is reserved for category/type badges (what kind of thing is this). Status badges (what state is it in) are always fill+dot or text-only+dot — never outlined, in any theme.
The Maintenance Law (1.13). Components consume the skin tokens — --hair, --r-ctl/--r-card/--r-nav, --tz-*/--tw-*, --tab-on, --badge-fill/--st-badge — never shape/type literals. A hardcoded 2px or "Inter" silently refuses to reshape under a skin.
Amber = unsaved edit · Red = real-world conflict (1.8). Yellow is the search-highlight only (1.7). Zebra stripes are for peer rows only, never nested children (1.13).

Themes & Skins

Two axes since 1.13: a theme (data-theme="light|dark") is the resolved token set; a skin (data-skin="blackops") layers one token-override block on top — no component forks. Black Ops is the default theme: it resolves to dark and re-tunes shape (0px radii, 1px hairlines), stroke-only badges, all-mono type, and the teal selection. Selection ships as the ThemeMenu popover: Light · Dark · Black Ops · System.

The intelligence gradient (--p-intel-gradient): Dusk Rose in light (#1861AF → #4338CA → #BE185D), the half-step blend in dark (#9FB6D4 → #929ADB → #D38AAD) — the brand's one gradient, reserved for AI/intelligence affordances. The Conditions map palette is the sole vivid exemption.
